blunder5...Bxd1
Falling for Légal's Mate
Taking the queen with 5...Bxd1 looks tempting, but White's knight on e5 was never really hanging — it was bait. The pin on the f3 knight was an illusion because White was willing to give up the queen. The right move was 5...Nxe5, simply recapturing and coming out a pawn up.
Recognising false pins & material traps
Before capturing a piece, always ask: what happens if I don't take it?

A coach who remembers you

The more you play, the better it knows you.

postgame doesn't review your games in isolation. It builds a picture of you as a player across every game you analyse — then tells you, in plain words, what to work on.

  • Recurring patterns — the mistakes you keep repeating, spotted across games.
  • What to work on next — one clear priority, not a wall of numbers.
  • Your real strengths — what you do well, so you know what to lean on.
  • A training plan you can tick off — concrete tasks with linked practice, so you always know your next step.
