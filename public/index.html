Falling for Légal's Mate
Taking the queen with 5...Bxd1 looks tempting, but White's knight on e5 was never really hanging — it was bait. The pin on the f3 knight was an illusion because White was willing to give up the queen. The right move was 5...Nxe5, simply recapturing and coming out a pawn up.
Recognising false pins & material traps
Before capturing a piece, always ask: what happens if I don't take it?
