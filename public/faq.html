Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Chess.com or Lichess analysis?
Chess.com and Lichess tell you a move was a "blunder" and show the engine's preferred move. postgame explains why your move was wrong, names the chess concept you missed (pin, fork, pawn break, etc.), and tells you specifically what to practise. It's the difference between a score and a lesson.
Where do I find my PGN?
On Chess.com: go to your game, click the share/export icon, and copy the PGN. On Lichess: open the game, click the menu (three dots), and select "Export PGN". You can also copy PGN from any chess app or database.
How long does analysis take?
Typically 2-5 minutes depending on game length. The engine evaluates every position, then the AI writes the coaching narrative. You can close the tab and come back — the analysis runs in the background.
How accurate is the coaching?
The engine analysis uses Stockfish, the world's strongest chess engine. The coaching narrative is generated by AI (Claude) based on the engine data and the actual board position. While the engine evaluation is highly accurate, the AI's tactical explanations can occasionally be imprecise. We're continuously improving this.
What chess rating range does this work for?
postgame works for all levels, but it's most useful for players rated roughly 600-2000. The coaching focuses on practical concepts and common patterns that drive improvement at these levels.
Do credits expire?
No. Purchased credits remain on your account until you use them.
What happens if an analysis fails?
If an analysis fails due to a technical error, your credit is automatically refunded.
Can I export my coaching report?
Yes. After analysis, click the "Export coaching report" button to download a Markdown file with the full narrative, critical moments, missed ideas, and study recommendations.
Can I review past analyses?
Yes. Click "My games" in the header to see all your past analyses. You can replay the game on the board and re-read the coaching commentary.
How do I get a refund?
Credits are non-refundable once purchased. Failed analyses are automatically refunded as credits. If you have a billing issue, contact us at info@post-game.net.
